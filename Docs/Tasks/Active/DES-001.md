## Deliverable
`SLICE_DESIGN.md`, slice-only:
1. Shift structure and length; win/fail; end-of-shift report fields.
2. Customer generator: archetypes used, how an intent is drawn (only intents the MIG-001 slice engine supports), constraints (budget, form), how customer lines are produced (templated; no NL parsing unless the slice engine has it).
3. Recommend interaction: what the player sees and picks, how the engine result is shown, how a choice is scored (satisfaction + recommendation-quality formulas, every constant in one table).
4. Sale → inventory decrement → what visibly changes in the store.
5. Queue/patience for the slice, or explicitly cut.
6. What the slice needs from GAME-001 (function signatures) and from ART-001 (anchors, interaction points).
7. A "cut from slice" list.

Mark each rule SOURCE-DERIVED (cite) or GAME-DESIGN DECISION. No invented store facts. UX-001 runs in parallel and may send you questions through the Hub; don't edit its files.
