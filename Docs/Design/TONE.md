# TONE — grounded, deadpan humour (tone pillar)

**Status:** adopted (DEC-101 (8), from Nolan's playtest 1 feedback, DEC-096; proposal `PROPOSAL_PLAYTEST1_LONGTERM.md` §8). **Read this before writing** any customer line, bark, toast flavour line, event, report line or coworker trait.

## The rule
The game is a straight simulation of a real shift. **The comedy comes from the simulation, and the writing plays it straight.** Funny things happen because the systems collide: a rush hits as the scale needs recalibrating, a customer asks for "the one from last time" with no other detail, the jar you need is the one that just sold out. Nobody in the game knows they are in a comedy. Nobody winks.

- **Grounded:** every situation could happen in a real store on a real day. Escalation is allowed, but each step stays believable.
- **Deadpan:** say it plainly, in the voice of someone who has seen it before. Understatement beats exaggeration.
- **Rare:** most lines are just work. A dry line lands because the ones around it are plain. Never joke-a-minute.
- **Earned by the sim:** prefer humour that the player caused or the state produced (timing, stock, queue, a persona's habit) over written-in gags.

## Never
- Corny jokes, puns, stoner clichés, "dude" voice, or winking at the player.
- **Real customers or staff as material** (no names, likenesses or stories from the real store; no PII, DEC-031 / CLAUDE.md).
- Mocking customers for their needs, conditions, age or medical status. The joke is the situation, never the person asking for help.
- Effect claims played for laughs: copy rule H1 still holds (needs may be stated by customers; system text never promises an effect).
- Humour that changes what the recommendation tool says or how it scores (DEC-001/002/006).

## Do / don't
| Situation | Do (played straight) | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Customer can't name the product | "It had a green label. Or the bag was green." | "Duuude, it was like… the green one, man 😂" |
| Rush plus equipment trouble | Toast: `Label printer: out of labels.` (then the rush keeps coming) | "Of COURSE the printer dies NOW. Classic!" |
| Strike toast flavour | "A minor's ID got through. Stores can be fined up to $2,000 per violation." | "Oopsie! Someone's getting a talking-to!" |
| Coworker trait (PS4) | "Fast at the register. Leaves the scale covered in shake." | "Speedy McMessypants" |
| Report line | "Longest wait: 6 min 40 s. Two people left during it." | "Yikes, your customers aged a year waiting!" |
| Quiet moment | Nothing. Silence is fine. | Filler jokes to fill the gap |

## For reviewers
A line passes if a tired budtender could say it on shift with a straight face, it points at something the simulation actually did, and it breaks none of the "Never" rules.
