# Progression Gate Stress Test

Synthetic profiles were sampled over six evidence shifts with independent per-competency SD=6. Promotion used the median-of-six + stability rule from `05_PROGRESSION_CAREER.md`.

Purpose: reject obvious dominant strategies before implementation.

Results are in `progression_gate_stress.csv`.

Interpretation:
- balanced mastery passes;
- throughput-only, accuracy-only, inventory-only and revenue-proxy profiles fail;
- a merely “good” balanced profile can reach Senior and sometimes Lead but does not coast automatically into AM/Manager.

This is a structural validation of gate design, not a prediction of real player promotion timing.
