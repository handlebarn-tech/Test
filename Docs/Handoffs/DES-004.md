## Caveats resolved
1. **Phase-rate renewal:** V2's harness already restarts the Gamma clock at every phase start. Realised counts are below λ·T (Standard per phase 0.05/2.35/5.38/0.91/6.30/2.40 vs nominal 0.24/2.70/5.76/1.20/6.48/2.70), and V2's 19.3 already includes that. Ruling: port the restart rule exactly, with a ±0.5 parity test in GAME-011.
2. **Registers:** the player runs one register, and the tables are the player's share. The other registers are ambient presentation (0–5 by phase, GAP-DAY-03) with no sim effect until PS4 staffing.
3. **Quiet-time compression:** structural clock map plus a hold-to-fast-forward (4×) with explicit start conditions and auto-stops. The arrival list is pre-generated in sim time, so fast-forward can't change who arrives (determinism test).
