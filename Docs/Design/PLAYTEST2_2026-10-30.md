> **Source:** Nolan's playtest-2 report, relayed to the Hub by the "Dispo Game product-owner communication" session on 2026-10-30 (paraphrased; no original file). **Build played:** `playtest2-741bb43`. **Decisions:** DEC-120..123. ## Findings (observed in build unless marked)
1. The exterior main doors must be transparent glass, with a higher-quality door asset and pass.
2. Exterior / outside world: confirm and preserve the existing plan. Do not duplicate it.
3. South side: too many registers, overcrowded, circulation too tight. Reduce the register count there.
4. The south-side counter/layout is still wrong, and Register 7 is not where it should be.
5. **Correction of Nolan's earlier call:** reducing or removing jars was a mistake. The store looked better and more accurate with jars filling the shelves. Restore appropriate jar density.
6. The emergency exit door near the sink / Register 6 has an open wall gap. Audit it and the surrounding geometry.
7. Full door audit: placement, dimensions, frame, materials, transparency, swing/opening behaviour, hardware, collision, wall sealing.
8. Many placeholder assets don't fit their spaces or intended use. They may stay visually temporary, but footprint, orientation, employee/customer side and spatial function must be correct.
9. The register area needs another evidence-based layout review: several assets face or occupy the customer side incorrectly.
10. Add a security guard / ID-check position at the front entrance.
11. ID checking does not function in the build. Required flow: (1) a security check at the door on entry; (2) a second ID verification by the budtender at the register.
12. Customers must not already stand at the register. They stay in the queue/wait state until the player calls next.
13. The recommendation interface is still too restrictive and keeps too little of the TFS tool's intelligence. Preserve natural-language intent, modifiers/negations and flexible recommendation behaviour, not a rigid menu.
14. *(Future, planned)* Packaged-product gameplay (vapes, edibles, concentrates, other non-deli products). FOH = active sales-floor stock; BOH = backstock feeding FOH.
