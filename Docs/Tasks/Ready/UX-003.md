## Deliverable
1. **Clock + phase:** the HUD game clock (08:00–22:00, display only, SHIFT_DAY §2), a phase banner on each boundary (name + one-line expectation, e.g. "Evening rush"), and the doors-closed state. At 22:00: a "Doors locked" banner, the clock keeps running past 22:00 while customers already inside are served, and Closing opens when the last one leaves (DEC-076). No countdown bars (DP §39).
2. **Fast-forward** (§7): the hold control, its available / unavailable look, and the auto-stop feedback (why it stopped: "customer arriving", "jar critical", "new phase"). Pause screen: everything frozen.
3. **Queue pressure:** one shared line from the entry, called up as registers free (DEC-079), with the player's-share count; a diegetic queue cue plus an optional HUD tint driven by PressureLevel / queue states (§6, §8). Presentation only.
4. **Stock cues** (§9a.3): LOW / CRITICAL tags on the jar and its TFS card, a trend arrow, a backstock-present marker, and EMPTY_WITH_BACKSTOCK vs SOLD OUT. Never a "RESTOCK NOW" prompt. **Status cues must not sit inside a TFS card's score/rank area** (§0A, as UX-005 did).
5. **Manager refill request** (§9a.4, DEC-080): a "Request refill" control at the register terminal and at the jar (3 s), with a refusal when there is no backstock. Show request markers (requested / with manager; queue order, no countdown), the jar leaving and returning (optional manager NPC), a house strain's second jar staying out, and REFILLING vs EMPTY_WITH_BACKSTOCK vs SOLD OUT. A customer line for waiting on a refill mid-service. Two jar slots per house strain on the wall (GAP-DAY-10).
6. **Opening** (§10): shift note card (day + expectation, no numbers) with the GM's register-assignment floor map (§5, DEC-079), and an "Open doors" button. **Closing:** the 3-item list (flag jars for the closing manager, deli BOH audit summary in 1 lb bags, clean scale) + Sign.
7. **Report v2** (§11): O16 section order with omitted sections absent, plus the Shift flow and Inventory sections. It sits alongside UX-005's SERVICE/FLOW/OPERATIONS; no TFS scores/ranks (DEC-052).
8. Keyboard/mouse mapping for each new control; one string table for `SliceLines.json`. Copy rule H1 (no effect claims).
