## Engine in brief
Parse (framing strip → strain/compare/like/terpene-rank routing → negations, 54 phrases, 287 keyword intents, conflict resolution, pain-functional cap → fuzzy) gives domain minimums and caps. Then: group batches by strain, average in-stock batches with an 8-week half-life, score 6 domains (tier weight × dominant bonus × BA / NF 2.7765), filter on stock, tier, exclusions, crit/caps, anxiety and price, rank on raw scores (primary ×1.5, cap-headroom penalty, cluster bonus, brand-tier nudge, like-X cosine, new-customer myrcene penalty), top 3, one relaxed retry. Freshness decay is dead code. There is no pairwise synergy. Feedback never touches ranking.
